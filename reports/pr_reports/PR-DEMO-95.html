Story summary

Apply an automatic discount at checkout based on order value and customer loyalty tier.

  • BR-1 Volume: < 100.00 → 0% · 100.00–499.99 → 5% · ≥ 500.00 → 10%
  • BR-2 Loyalty: STANDARD → 0% · SILVER → 2% · GOLD → 5%
  • BR-3: volume + loyalty, capped at 15%
  • BR-4: BigDecimal, HALF_UP, 2 decimal places

Confluence: PRD - E-commerce platform (§4.4 says the order summary itemizes subtotal and discount; it defines no discount rules).
