Story summary

Customers can cancel a placed order and give a reason.

  • BR-1: every order has a status: PLACED or CANCELLED
  • BR-2: only a PLACED order can be cancelled
  • BR-3: reason required, not blank, at most 255 characters
  • BR-4: reason and cancelledAt (from an injected Clock) are recorded
